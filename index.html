SPACE STIM

A spacer's life in the Galactic Empire. You own one old light hauler, parked in Bay 3 of the Terminus City spaceport, at the very edge of the Galaxy. Where you take her is your business.

An unofficial fan-made game set in the world of Isaac Asimov's novel Foundation. Places and politics follow the book; all characters you meet, dialogue, and minor worlds are original to this game and are marked as such on the chart.
